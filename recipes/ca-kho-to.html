Ca Kho To

Description

This is my absolute favorite dish in the whole entire world. It's so warm and comforting and very easy to make. These types of dishes (braised) is a staple in Vietnamese households. Most people enjoy the braised pork, but the tenderness of the fish makes this dish even better in my opinion.

This recipe uses swai fish, but I've seen this made with salmon fillets as well. Experiment with the type of fish you use or just use your favorite! I would say though, that white fishes work the best.

Ingredients

  • shallots
  • salt
  • pepper
  • palm sugar coloring paste
  • gochugaru
  • cocorico (coconut soda) or water
  • swai fish
  • whole red peppers

Steps

  1. Season fish fillets with a little salt for about 30 minutes. Place fish fillets into pot that fit it snuggly.
  2. Heat up the pot (with the fish inside). Add in shallots, palm sugar coloring paste, gochugaru, whole red peppers, and cocorico (enough to slightly cover the fish).
  3. Lightly mix until even. Simmer for about 1 hour or until fish is cooked and the liquid is reduced.
  4. Garnish with pepper.
