Butter Chicken

Description

Butter chicken is a classic indian dish that is probably the most known by the general public. It is also the first Indian dish that I made from scratch. The first time I made it, I used a traditional recipe where I marinated the chicken beforehand with yogurt and lime. I also made the tomato sauce from scratch with fresh tomatoes and soaked cashews. Although it was very good, it took a lot of effort and time to make.

This recipe is a simplification of the original butter chicken recipe that I have made a couple of times. Unfortunately, I did find that this "shortcut" recipe tasted better and the texture was more desirable. Please proceed with caution as this is not close to authentic at all. It is just a girl who likes Indian food making her cravings possible with less effort. Serve this recipe with some rice or *fresh* naan.

Ingredients

  • diced chicken thighs (skinless and boneless if you want)
  • unflavored greek yogurt
  • lime juice
  • minced garlic
  • minced onion
  • minced ginger
  • dried fenugreek leaves
  • garam masala
  • oil or ghee
  • tomato sauce (no herbs except garlic and onion if possible)
  • heavy cream
  • butter
  • salt
  • pepper
  • sugar

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken thighs with yogurt, lime juice, garlic, onion, giner, fenugreek leaves, and garam masala for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Grill or pan fry chicken until browned. Remove chicken from the pan.
  3. Heat up some oil or ghee in same pan. Stir in more garlic, onion, and garlic until fragrant and onion is translucent. Add garam masala and fenugreek leaves. Continue stirring seasonings are soaked in.
  4. Add tomato sauce. Heat until bubbling. Taste and season with salt, pepper, sugar, garam masala as needed.
  5. Add chicken back in. Simmer until chicken is cooked through, about 30 mintues to 1 hour.
  6. Add in heavy cream and butter until creaminess level reached.
